What UpTo is

Friends, not followers.

Remember when social media was just your friends? UpTo is that: a quiet feed of what the people you actually know are actually doing. No brands, no strangers, no infinite scroll engineered to keep you refreshing.

01

One post a day

Share a moment — text, a photo, or a few — and that’s it until tomorrow. It keeps UpTo something you check, not something you scroll.

02

Invite-only by design

You connect by sharing your personal invite link. Posts, photos, and comments go only to friends you have accepted — no public feed, no follower counts, no discovery surface.

03

The feed has an end

Newest first, every time — no ranking, no “suggested for you.” When you have seen what your friends shared, UpTo says so. Close the app and go live your day.
