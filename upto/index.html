What UpTo is

A quieter way to keep up.

UpTo is a small social app for the handful of people you actually know. You post once a day, your friends see it, and that is the whole product.

01

One post a day

A single post per day, on your device’s time zone. It keeps the app something you check, not something you scroll.

02

Friends only

Posts, photos, and comments go to people whose connection requests you have accepted. There is no public feed and no discovery surface.

03

No ads, no algorithm

Your feed is in order, from your friends. UpTo carries no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDK, and no third-party trackers.
